# Office joke candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters, office comedy. Scored
on the laugh, head to head.

1. Lifecycle: "The standup became an email, the email became
   a thread, and the thread became a meeting." -- the office
   circle of life; the ending could close the loop back to a
   standup for the full cycle.
2. Reply-all: "Someone hit reply-all to 4,000 people to say
   'please stop replying all', and 400 people replied all to
   agree." -- documentary realism.
3. Calendar: "My calendar has a meeting to plan the meeting
   about reducing meetings. I have accepted all three." --
   well-shaped, familiar family.
4. Printer: "The printer is the only employee who says
   exactly what it thinks: PC LOAD LETTER, and honestly,
   same." -- nerdy, older reference.
5. Out of office: "His out-of-office says he'll reply slower
   than usual, which the team agrees is a bold claim." --
   dry, smaller.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The draft
stopped at the meeting; the shipped line closes the loop --
the meeting schedules a standup -- so the joke is no longer a
chain but a cycle, which is the actual horror of office life
and the bigger laugh.
