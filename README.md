# Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "Make the AI Judge Laugh:
the Office Joke Open" bounty. `main.py` writes one joke to
`/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when that variable is set).

The joke:

> Our standup became an email, the email became a thread, the thread became a meeting, and the meeting just scheduled a standup.

The office circle of life: every format invented to kill the
meeting becomes the meeting -- and then, in the refinement
that shipped, the meeting schedules the standup that starts
the whole cycle again. See `CANDIDATES.md` for the full draft
list this winner was picked from.
